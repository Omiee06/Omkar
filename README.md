# Create a Google Account
1. Open the `Chrome` browser.
2. Search for [Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com/?pli=1)
![image alt](https://github.com/Omiee06/Omkar/blob/51f89047047ffad7ca5c3aba8cc151c9a1394e3a/google%20account.png)
***
3. Click **Create an account**.
![image alt](https://github.com/Omiee06/Omkar/blob/bd321cbe9f7d5b70347f6e887e404be01c35d218/Create%20an%20Account.png)
***
4. Enter your first and last Name in the appropriate text box.
5. Click **Next**.
![image alt](https://github.com/Omiee06/Omkar/blob/51c7480221bf52da95654bb94fe4fd0a7887018b/Type%20Name.png)
**Tip:** Choose your preferred language.
***
6. Enter your **birthday and gender**.
7. Click **Next**.
![image alt](https://github.com/Omiee06/Omkar/blob/5f8d06b5ce8abc6b3728d09edaa36532f041afea/Basic%20Info.png)
***
8. Choose a suggested Gmail address or select **Create a Gmail address**.
9. If you choose to create a Gmail address, type your new Gmail ID in the text box.
10. Click **Next**.
![image alt](https://github.com/Omiee06/Omkar/blob/f5ad80d286c0fec6889e107a4f50a1b409432c62/MAIL%20INFO.png)
***
11. Enter a password.
12. Confirm password.
13. Click ** Next **.
![image alt](https://github.com/Omiee06/Omkar/blob/f508c75f5484ac90ff66b1fc6d84258c08c23fa8/password.png)
***
14. Scan the QR code from your mobile device.
![image alt](
15. Verify your device.
    ***
16. Add a recovery Gmail ID, if available, and click **Next**, or click **Skip**.
***
17. Review your account.
18. Click **Next.**
***
19. Read **Privacy and Terms**.
20. Click **I agree**.
23. Your account is created.
