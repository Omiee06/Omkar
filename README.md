# Create a Google Account
* Open the `Chrome` browser.
* Search [Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com/?pli=1)
![image alt](https://github.com/Omiee06/Omkar/blob/51f89047047ffad7ca5c3aba8cc151c9a1394e3a/google%20account.png)
***
* Click Create an account
![image alt](https://github.com/Omiee06/Omkar/blob/bd321cbe9f7d5b70347f6e887e404be01c35d218/Create%20an%20Account.png)
***
* Enter your first and last Name in the appropriate text box.
* Click **Next**.
![image alt](https://github.com/Omiee06/Omkar/blob/51c7480221bf52da95654bb94fe4fd0a7887018b/Type%20Name.png)
**Tip** - You can choose your preferred language.
***
* Enter your birthdate.
* Choose Gender.
* Click **Next**.
![image alt](https://github.com/Omiee06/Omkar/blob/5f8d06b5ce8abc6b3728d09edaa36532f041afea/Basic%20Info.png)
***
* Choose a suggested Gmail address or select **Create a Gmail address**.
* If you choose to create a Gmail address, type your new Gmail ID in the text box.
* Click **Next**.
![image alt](https://github.com/Omiee06/Omkar/blob/f5ad80d286c0fec6889e107a4f50a1b409432c62/MAIL%20INFO.png)
***
